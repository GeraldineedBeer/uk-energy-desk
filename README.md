# UK Energy Desk

Daily UK electricity, gas and water market update for energy managers and brokers: live prices, the best times to buy, invoice and pass-through charges, supplier rates and an AI assistant.

**Open or install:** https://geraldineedbeer.github.io/uk-energy-desk/

Works in any browser. Install it from the browser (Install button on Android/computers, Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone). No app store needed.

- Live prices come directly from Elexon BMRS, Octopus Energy and NESO Carbon Intensity.
- The daily briefing and chatbot are served by the UK Energy Desk API.
